{% comment %} A bar of bulk actions, above a list: « Mon corpus » (#287), the references and « Cité par » of a publication (#408). The checkboxes of the rows belong to this form by their form attribute (each row already holds forms of its own, and forms do not nest). One button per action (name="action"), removal confirmed in a popover. Several bars can share a page: every id derives from form_id. static/js/bulk_actions.js counts the rows checked, checks them all and disables the buttons while none is; without it, the server says when nothing is checked. Context: form_id, reading_statuses, tag_max_length, bulk_tags (the user's tags, suggested as the tag is typed); with action, the URL the form is sent to (else « Mon corpus »'s); with next, the page to go back to (else this one); with add, a button « Ajouter à mon corpus », which gives the tag and status chosen too; with collapsed (#410), the script folds the bar into its first line while nothing is checked, and unfolds it for good at the first box checked (without it, the bar stays unfolded). The controls and the help are in data-bulk-more, the part folded. {% endcomment %} {% load ui %}
